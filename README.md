# Evaluating the Implementation of Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY) in Rural Uttar Pradesh

## 📄 Project Overview
This research project examines the implementation and impact of the Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY) in the rural areas of Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh, India. Conducted by MBA students from the School of Management Studies at MNNIT Allahabad, the study aimed to assess the awareness, accessibility, transparency, and effectiveness of PMGKAY among rural beneficiaries.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Assess rural residents' awareness of PMGKAY entitlements.
- Evaluate accessibility and transparency of food grain distribution.
- Analyze grievance redressal mechanisms.
- Determine the impact on food security and poverty alleviation.

## 🧪 Methodology
- **Sample Size**: 58 rural respondents from Gajyani Saraiya and Pasiyapur villages in Prayagraj.
- **Sampling Technique**: Random sampling.
- **Data Collection**: Structured questionnaire (both English and Hindi).
- **Tools**: Statistical analysis using SPSS.

## 📊 Key Findings
- High awareness of PMGKAY, but gaps exist in accessibility and transparency.
- No significant relationship found between income and grievances, food security, or financial impact.
- Grievance redressal mechanisms were weak and underutilized.
- PMGKAY helped alleviate hunger but needs better execution and monitoring.

## 📌 Conclusion
PMGKAY was effective during the COVID-19 crisis in ensuring food security for rural populations. However, challenges such as awareness gaps, irregular distribution, and poor grievance handling must be addressed to improve its long-term impact.
